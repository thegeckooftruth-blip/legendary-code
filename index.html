My Coding Process

1. I start by brainstorming and writing down ideas based on your concept.

2. Next, I build out all the essential structural sections your site needs.

3. Then I style everything using modern CSS to create a crisp look.

4. After that, I work directly with you to adjust anything you want changed.

The full process usually takes around 3–4 hours.
