My Coding Process

I start by brainstorming and writing down ideas based on the concept you describe.
Next, I build out all the sections you want your site to include.
Then I style everything using CSS to create a clean, modern look.
After that, I work with you to adjust anything you want changed.
The full process usually takes around 3–4 hours.
